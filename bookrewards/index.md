# BookRewards Support

Need help or have feedback? Email [info@chrisramos.com](mailto:info@chrisramos.com) and we'll get back to you.

## How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to **My Challenges**, scroll to the bottom, and tap **Delete account**. This permanently removes your account and all of your challenges, readers, quizzes, and reading logs.

## How do I delete just one challenge?

On **My Challenges**, tap **Delete** on that challenge's card.

## Does BookRewards handle real money?

No. The reward is a note you write (for example, "movie night" or "$10"). Any payout happens outside the app.

## My reader's quiz didn't load

Quizzes are generated over the internet. Check your connection and try again. If it keeps failing, email us with the book title.
