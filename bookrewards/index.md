# BookRewards Support

Need help or have feedback? Email [chrisramos@me.com](mailto:chrisramos@me.com) and we'll get back to you.

## I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, type your email address and tap **Forgot password?**. We'll email you a link to choose a new password (check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive in a few minutes). If you signed up with Apple, there is no password: tap the Apple button to sign in. If you can't get into your email either, write to us and we'll help.

## How do I delete my account?

Open the app's home screen, scroll to the bottom of the **Challenges** tab, and tap **Delete account**. This permanently removes your account and all of your challenges, readers, chat questions, quizzes, and reading logs.

## How do I rename or delete a reader?

On the home screen, open the **Readers** tab and tap the reader. Tap **Rename** to change their name. To remove them, tap **Delete** at the bottom of their page. This permanently deletes the reader along with all of their challenges and reading history.

## How do I delete just one challenge?

On the home screen's **Challenges** tab, tap **Delete** on that challenge's card.

## Does BookRewards handle real money?

No. The reward is a note you write (for example, "movie night" or "$10"). Any payout happens outside the app.

## How does a checkpoint work?

When your reader reaches a checkpoint, tap **Start Chat** and hand them the device. The challenger (you) then taps **Challenger: show the questions** and asks a few of the discussion questions out loud. When you're happy they know the story, tap **Talked it through**. If not, tap **Not yet** and try again after more reading.

## Can I give my reader a quiz instead of a chat?

Yes. When you create a challenge, choose **My own quiz** under **How to check each checkpoint**. After creating it, tap **Edit** on the challenge, open **Quizzes**, and write multiple-choice questions for each checkpoint, marking the correct answer. The app marks the answers; no AI is involved.

## Can I write my own chat questions?

Yes. On the home screen's **Challenges** tab, tap **My chat questions**. Type a question, choose which kind of book it suits (or any book), and optionally limit it to the last checkpoint. Your questions are mixed in with the built-in ones, and you can delete any of them later.
