# HornToad Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

HornToad does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

- **No account.** You do not sign in or create a profile.
- **No data collection.** The app does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or usage data.
- **No tracking or advertising.** The app contains no ads, analytics, or third-party tracking tools.
- **Works offline.** All signal information and sounds are built into the app. It does not need an internet connection.
- **External links.** The "About and sources" screen links to public government websites. If you open one, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Your choice of Inland or International rules is kept only while the app is open and is not stored or sent anywhere.
