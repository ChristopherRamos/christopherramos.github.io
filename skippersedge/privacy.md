# Skippers Edge Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Skippers Edge does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

- **No account.** You do not sign in or create a profile.
- **No data collection.** The app does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or usage data.
- **No tracking or advertising.** The app contains no ads, analytics, or third-party tracking tools.
- **Works offline.** All calculation happens on your device. The app does not connect to the internet to work.
- **Saved on your device.** The level of play, the adjustments and game rules you choose, and any calls you log in the game log are saved on your device so they are there next time. They never leave it unless you choose to export the game log, which sends it only to the place you pick in your phone's share sheet. If you delete the app, that saved information is deleted with it.
